# SEO Content Readability Guidelines

To prevent "Content: Readability Difficult" and "Content: Readability Very Difficult" issues in future pages or templates, follow these guidelines:

## Sentence Length
- Prefer short and medium-length sentences.
- Aim for 25 words or fewer per sentence where practical.
- Break very long sentences into two or more natural sentences. 
- Avoid stringing clauses together with excessive conjunctions (e.g., "and", "but", "so").

## Paragraph Structure
- Break large blocks of text into smaller paragraphs.
- Prefer 2–4 sentence paragraphs.
- Use clear topic-focused sections.
- Utilize bullets or numbered steps where appropriate, as they naturally break up dense text.
- Use tables where they genuinely improve understanding.

## Language and Vocabulary
- Use simpler wording where the meaning remains accurate.
- Replace unnecessarily complicated wording with clear alternatives.
- **Do NOT remove necessary legal or trademark terminology.** Terms like "trademark registration," "intellectual property," and "trademark application" must be retained.
- Reduce unnecessary filler, excessive adjectives, and marketing jargon (e.g., "ultimate," "comprehensive," "exhaustive").
- Avoid unnatural sentence construction or repeated keyword-heavy text simply to boost keyword density.

## Template and Dynamic Content
- When generating content for location/city pages, avoid creating unnaturally long sentences by blindly interpolating variables.
- Example to **avoid**: "Having a unique identity is very important in today's market and whether your business is local or national, getting trademark registration in [Location] is a key legal step that secures your brand ownership."
- **Improved**: "A unique brand identity is vital today. Trademark registration in [Location] is a key legal step. It secures your brand ownership."
- If the same paragraph is generated for hundreds of pages, fix the reusable template source rather than manually overriding each page.
- Do NOT concatenate text items into one string without proper punctuation. If displaying lists or grids, ensure the content does not appear to Screaming Frog as one giant run-on sentence.

## Testing
- After applying changes to a template, verify the Flesch Reading Ease score on representative pages.
- Ensure the changes improve genuine readability for the target audience without gaming the SEO metrics.
